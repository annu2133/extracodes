Here is what you get when you work with NIKANU WEB SOLUTIONS.

What You Get

One team for your whole online growth

You do not need many agencies. From the first idea to the final lead, we plan, build and improve everything in one place, in simple words.

Customized Digital Marketing Strategy

Every business is different, so we do not use one plan for everyone. We first learn about your business, your customers and your goal. Then we pick the channels that suit you best and fit your budget.

You always know what we are doing and why we are doing it.

Website Design & Development

Your website is often the first thing a customer sees. We build websites that look clean, load fast and work well on phones. Every page is made to help visitors understand you and contact you easily.

We work with WordPress and custom builds, and we can also set up your online store.

SEO & Search Engine Optimization

When people search for what you offer, we help your business show up. We work on keywords, page content, site speed, technical fixes and link building, so the right customers can find you on Google.

SEO takes time, but the results build up and last.

Social Media Marketing & Branding

We help your brand look and sound the same everywhere it shows up. From your profile to your posts, we build a brand people recognise and trust, and we link it to real business goals.

We also manage your community, so your audience feels heard.

Creative Content & Graphic Design

Good ideas need a good look. Our team writes clear content and designs posts, banners and graphics that catch attention and say the right thing in simple words.

Blogs, videos and copy are all made to educate, engage and convert.

Paid Advertising & Lead Generation

We run ads on platforms like Google and Meta with one clear aim: bring in people who may really buy. We watch your spend, test different ads and keep improving, so your money works harder.

Our focus is on qualified leads, not empty clicks.

Data-Driven Performance & Analytics

We do not guess. We track what is working and what is not, and we use that data to make better decisions. Every month you get a simple report that shows where your leads are coming from.

No hidden charges and no confusing words.

Result-Oriented Marketing Approach

Our focus is on outcomes that matter to your business, such as enquiries, calls and sales. We are honest about what is possible. We cannot promise exact results, but we do promise clear effort and clear communication.

We test, report and scale what works best for you.
